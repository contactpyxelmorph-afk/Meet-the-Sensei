SELECTED LABORATORY WORK

Fragile parts.
Meaningful signals.

Fracture resistance of cured binder-jetted metal parts

Under Prof. Paolo Parenti in the Laboratory of Mechanical Technology, I designed and ran a test workflow with custom specimens, CNC impact paths, a piezoelectric dynamometer and NI 9234 acquisition.

Three-axis force analysis separated fracture peaks from motion artifacts, translating bending-dominated failure into practical force envelopes for automated depowdering and handling.

~2.3 MPaEstimated ultimate tensile strength
